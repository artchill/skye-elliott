Meet the Author

Skye Elliott

Novelist · Observer · Storyteller

Skye Elliott writes from the shadows where most refuse to look. With a keen psychological eye for the unspoken rituals and private moments that quietly define us, her work peers through the curtained windows of human experience — exploring the tension between intimacy and observation, between the secrets we keep and the truths we cannot hide. Her debut novel, Windows, charts the moral and emotional terrain of a watcher who knows more than he should, asking readers to reckon with their own thresholds of judgment, desire, and discretion.

A lifelong student of the human condition, Skye is drawn to characters who live at the margins of acceptability — those whose longings complicate easy verdicts. Her prose moves with the patience of someone who has spent years watching without being seen, attentive to small gestures and longer silences. She believes the most honest fiction lives in the spaces we are taught to ignore, and Windows is her invitation to step closer, to look longer, and to consider what we find there in the dark.
